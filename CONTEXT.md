# FocusQuote

The shared language for FocusQuote — a Chrome extension and backend for capturing web quotes and running goal-oriented focus sessions with AI assistance. This glossary is the source of truth for what domain terms mean; prefer these words over the alternatives listed under _Avoid_.

## Language

**Session**:
The consent-and-capture window during which the coach observes what the user studies: a period of focused work toward a Goal, opened by the user, throughout which the extension tracks the pages visited (URLs) so the coach can work from them. The timer is incidental; the session's reason to exist is that it gates when capture happens — no session, no capture.
_Avoid_: Pomodoro, timer

**History**:
The view of past focus sessions surfaced for later review (today, the sessions older than the last week).
_Avoid_: Archive, Storage

**Topic**:
A research subject that groups focus sessions sharing a theme and owns a Vault. The AI suggests its name, but a Topic has a stable identity independent of that name.
_Avoid_: Category, folder

**Vault**:
The information a user keeps under a Topic — text notes, saved URLs, and screenshots — the record of what they already hold and know on that Topic.
_Avoid_: Storage, archive

**Vault item**:
A single curated piece in a Topic's Vault: a note, a saved URL, or a screenshot. Curated by the user, distinct from the pages a session tracks automatically.
_Avoid_: Attachment, resource

**Tracked visit**:
A page the extension recorded automatically during a session for distraction analysis (`SessionUrl`). Telemetry, not curated knowledge — promoting one into the Vault turns it into a Vault item.
_Avoid_: Visit, history entry, session URL

**Quote**:
An important piece of text a user captures from a page to keep. Portable knowledge, filed globally in the Tag graph — independent of any Session or Topic.
_Avoid_: Highlight, snippet, clip

**Tag**:
A subject a Quote is filed under. Tags form a graph: a Tag may have several parent Tags, and a Quote may carry several Tags. The AI assigns and maintains them; a Tag a user creates or edits is pinned and the AI leaves it alone. A Tag has a stable identity independent of its name.
_Avoid_: Folder, category, label, node

**Consolidation**:
The user-triggered pass ("Tidy now") in which the AI restructures the Tag graph — merging duplicates, renaming, re-parenting, splitting — and re-files only the Quotes those changes touch. Distinct from the lightweight tagging that happens automatically when a Quote is saved, which only attaches a Quote and creates Tags.
_Avoid_: Cleanup, reorg, sync

**Conversation**:
A persistent Quote+AI chat scoped to a single article (page URL). Highlighted passages are added as context to individual turns; they don't start separate chats. Persists locally and syncs when signed in.
_Avoid_: Thread, chat session

**Goal**:
The forward-looking intent a user sets for a Session — what they mean to accomplish. User-authored.
_Avoid_: Title, task

**Title**:
A short retrospective label for a Session, generated by AI from its Topic and tracked visits. Shown when no Goal was set; never overwrites a Goal.
_Avoid_: Goal, name
